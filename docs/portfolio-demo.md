# Steadier: 90-second portfolio walkthrough

**Audience:** a recruiter or engineer evaluating the prototype. **Use only synthetic demo data** in screenshots and video.

## 0–15 s · The problem
Show the Today dashboard. Explain: “A list of goals is not a plan. Steadier works out what might actually fit into available time while leaving room for rest.”

## 15–35 s · Competing goals
Open Goals. Show deadlines, remaining-effort ranges and feasibility labels. Change a goal's deadline or workload in the what-if simulator. Explain that the estimate compares required effort with bounded, conservative capacity; no change is saved until explicitly applied.

## 35–60 s · From plan to action
Return to Today. Show a calendar commitment, choose a planning mode and generate. Demonstrate a smaller task variant or **Make easier**, plus a pinned task and an available window. Explain that this is deterministic scheduling, not generative AI or an optimality claim.

## 60–75 s · Human-centred tracking
Show the daily observations and routines, with intentionally missing fields. Explain that absence is not treated as zero, and task completion does not alter the wellbeing heatmap. Screen time is manually entered, not read automatically from iOS.

## 75–90 s · Engineering evidence
Show the repository's pure TypeScript engines, automated tests, architecture notes and privacy boundaries. Finish with: “I built this to explore explainable trade-offs between workload, time and energy; current work focuses on device validation and product iteration.”
